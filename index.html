Engineered for Zero Failure

Built with Rust and modern Svelte 5 runes for absolute reliability under pressure.

01 / GRAPHICS LOOP

Isolated 60 FPS Engine

The audience window runs in a dedicated thread. Heavy library imports, database queries, and slide edits never cause the projector to drop a frame.

02 / HARDWARE VERIFICATION

Physical Screen Capture

Screen View mode uses native desktop capture (ScreenCaptureKit & DXGI) to mirror exactly what the physical projector is showing right on your desk.

03 / CONTENT TOOLS

Auto-Splitter & Grammar Check

Paste long scripture chapters or songs and automatically split them into 2-line or 4-line slides. Built-in grammar audit catches typos before you go live.

04 / BROADCAST STREAM

OBS Transparent Overlays

Integrated broadcast web server provides alpha-transparent lower thirds, news tickers, and speaker cards directly to OBS Studio or vMix.

05 / STAGE MONITOR

Stage Confidence & Nursery Alerts

Confidence monitors show high-contrast lyrics, clock, and countdown timers to the stage, with discreet nursery alert tickers for the auditorium.

06 / RESILIENCE

Offline-First & Crash Recovery

Zero internet required. All data is saved instantly to embedded SQLite WAL. If power is interrupted, 1-click resumes your exact slide in under 2 seconds.

First-Run & Installation Guidance

macOS Gatekeeper Note (Direct Download)

Because Stagelite is distributed directly without the Mac App Store, macOS may show a prompt: "Stagelite cannot be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software". Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway, or run xattr -cr /Applications/Stagelite.app in Terminal.

Windows SmartScreen Notice

On Windows 10/11, SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC" on brand-new builds. Click More info and then click Run anyway.

Linux AppImage Execution

Ensure execute permissions are enabled before launching: chmod +x Stagelite-Linux-x64.AppImage
