Engineered for Zero Failure

Built with Rust and modern Svelte 5 runes for unbeatable reliability in live environments.

⚡

Isolated 60 FPS Render Loop

The audience window runs in a dedicated thread. Heavy library imports, database queries, and slide edits never cause the projector to drop a single frame.

🖥️

Physical Screen Verification

Screen View mode uses native desktop capture (ScreenCaptureKit & DXGI) to mirror exactly what the physical projector or video wall is displaying directly on your operator desk.

✍️

Auto-Splitter & Grammar Check

Paste long scripture chapters or songs and automatically split them into clean 2-line or 4-line slides. Built-in grammar audit catches typos before you go live.

📡

Transparent OBS Broadcast Overlays

Integrated broadcast web server provides alpha-transparent lower thirds, news tickers, and speaker cards directly to OBS Studio, vMix, or ATEM switchers.

⏱️

Stage Confidence & Nursery Alerts

Dedicated confidence monitors show high-contrast lyrics, clock, and countdown timers to the stage, with discreet nursery alert tickers for the auditorium.

🛡️

Offline-First & Instant Crash Recovery

Zero internet required. All data is saved instantly to embedded SQLite WAL. If power is interrupted, 1-click resumes your exact slide in under 2 seconds.

First-Run & Installation Guidance

🍏 macOS Gatekeeper Guidance (Ad-Hoc / Community Build)

Because Stagelite is distributed directly without the Mac App Store, macOS Gatekeeper may show: "Stagelite cannot be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software".

To launch: Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the Security section, and click Open Anyway. Or simply run this one-time command in Terminal: xattr -cr /Applications/Stagelite.app

🪟 Windows SmartScreen Notice

On Windows 10/11, Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC" on newly released binaries. Click More info and then click Run anyway.

🐧 Linux AppImage Execution

Grant execute permissions before launching: chmod +x Stagelite-Linux-x64.AppImage
