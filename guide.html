Stagelite Operator Manual Section 1: Quick Start & Core Concepts

1. Quick Start: The 3-Minute Volunteer Guide

If you are arriving at the AV desk 10 minutes before a service starts, follow these four simple steps:

1
Launch Stagelite and Select Your Run-Sheet: Stagelite starts in Master Studio mode (⌘1 / Ctrl+1). In the left column, click the first item of the service (e.g. Welcome Announcements or Song 1).
2
Verify Screen Connection: Look at the top right of your screen. If the badge reads ○ STANDBY, the output window is closed. Click the blue Open Audience Screen button in the right-hand panel. The badge will change to glowing red ● ON AIR.
3
Click Any Slide to Project: In the center slide matrix, click Verse 1. It projects immediately to the congregation screen. Press the Space key or → arrow key to advance through verses.
4
Remember the Two Panic Buttons: If the speaker starts talking before the slide is ready, or if an incorrect slide appears:
• Tap B (Blank) to fade the screen to pure black. Tap B again to restore it.
• Tap Esc (Clear) to remove all words while keeping the clean background graphic.
The Golden Rule of Live Presentation
Never hesitate to press B (Blank) if you are unsure which slide to show. A black screen looks intentional and dignified; frantic clicking on the audience screen distracts the entire room.

2. Stagelite Core Concepts in 60 Seconds

Stagelite organizes all presentation material into four clean, predictable layers:

Program (Run-Sheet)

The ordered playlist for the entire service (e.g. Welcome → 3 Worship Songs → Scripture → Sermon Notes → Benediction).

Show

A single reusable content deck (such as the hymn Amazing Grace or a guest speaker's 10-slide teaching deck).

Slide

A single visual card with formatted lyrics, bible verses, backgrounds, and auto-fit typographic styles.

Audience Output

An isolated 60 FPS hardware renderer that projects to projectors or TVs without stuttering or freezing.

Stagelite Operator Manual Section 2: Master Studio Interface Tour

2. Master Studio Interface Tour

The Master Studio (⌘1 / Ctrl+1) is designed so you never have to hunt through menus during a live service:

The Three Working Zones Explained:

  • Zone 1 (Left — Service Schedule): Displays your planned running order. Shows how many slides each item has, and highlights the currently active show in bold blue.
  • Zone 2 (Center — Slide Matrix): Shows a large preview of the active slide at the top, and interactive thumbnail cards of all slides in the song at the bottom. Click any card to switch immediately.
  • Zone 3 (Right — Audience Output & Tactical Transport): Features a live miniature mirror of what the congregation sees, next/previous buttons, panic controls, nursery alerts, and countdown timers.

Stagelite Operator Manual Section 3: The 5 Dashboard Modes

3. The 5 Dashboard Modes

Stagelite offers 5 purpose-built modes for different operational situations:

Mode 1: Studio Mode (⌘1 / Ctrl+1) Primary Desk

The all-in-one console. Includes the run-sheet, slide matrix, and live audience mirror. Supports switching between Instant Click-to-Live and professional Studio Mode (Preview / TAKE).

Mode 2: Displays (⌘2 / Ctrl+2) Hardware Verification

Uses native desktop capture to show live video thumbnails of all connected physical monitors. Lets you record presentation output directly to video (MP4/WebM) with live FPS telemetry, or capture 1080p snapshots.

Mode 3: Planning (⌘3 / Ctrl+3) Mid-Week Programming

Build and re-order services during the week. Search millions of free CC0 backgrounds and photos from Openverse, import scripture, and run an automated grammar audit to catch typos before the event.

Mode 4: WYSIWYG Slide Editor (⌘4 / Ctrl+4) Authoring & Formatting

Full typographic control: font sizing (18px–96px), line height, text shadows, alignment, and auto-splitter (pastes whole songs and breaks them into 2-line or 4-line slides). Includes Publish Mode to edit privately while live.

Mode 5: Presenter View (⌘5 / Ctrl+5) Minimalist Volunteer Mode

A high-contrast, distraction-free view with huge Next/Previous buttons, large status indicators, and an emergency in-place typo corrector for last-second fixes.

Pro Operator Tip: Instant Mode vs. Preview / TAKE
In Studio Mode, toggle the switch under STUDIO MODE to Active. Now, clicking slides stages them safely in the amber box. You can read them over and press Enter to take them live when the speaker speaks.

Stagelite Operator Manual Section 4: Step-by-Step Operator Recipes

4. Step-by-Step Operator Recipes

Recipe 1: Presenting Worship Lyrics with Confidence

1
Click the song title in the Service Schedule (left panel).
2
Click Verse 1 to send the first lyrics live to the audience.
3
Listen to the vocalist. As they reach the last syllable of the current line, press Space or →. Advancing early allows the congregation to read ahead naturally.
4
If the leader repeats a chorus out of order, click the Chorus card directly in the center grid. Stagelite jumps straight to it without scrolling through other slides.

Recipe 2: Staging Slides Safely (Studio Mode / TAKE)

1
In the right column, toggle Studio Mode to Active.
2
Click any slide in the grid. It appears in the amber Next to Air staging box, but the audience screen does not change.
3
When ready, press Enter (or click TAKE LIVE). The slide dissolves smoothly to the screen.

Recipe 3: Nursery Alerts & Speaker Countdown Timers

Pushing a Nursery Alert Banner
  1. In Master Studio (right panel), find QUICK ALERT TICKER.
  2. Type the child's tag number (e.g. Parent #314).
  3. Click Push Alert. A discreet banner appears across the bottom of the audience screen.
  4. Click Clear to dismiss once the parent arrives.
Speaker Countdown Timer
  1. In Master Studio, find EVENT TIMERS.
  2. Set the sermon length (e.g. 30:00).
  3. Click Start. The timer displays on the Stage Monitor facing the speaker.
  4. If the speaker exceeds time, the timer turns amber with negative elapsed time (e.g. -02:15).

Recipe 4: Multi-Screen Routing & Identify Test Bars

1
In the AUDIENCE section of the right panel, view the target buttons: ALL, EXT, 1, 2.
2
Click EXT to project exclusively to your external projector while keeping your laptop display dedicated to the control console.
3
Click Identify to flash SMPTE color test patterns and confirm projector cabling.

Stagelite Operator Manual Section 5: Broadcast, Stage Display & Recovery

5. Broadcast Graphics, Stage Displays & Recovery

Broadcast Graphics & OBS Studio Live-Streaming

Stagelite provides an integrated broadcast graphics server for YouTube and Facebook live streams:

1
Click Broadcast Graphics in the Master Studio toolbar.
2
Choose a template: Lower-Thirds, News Tickers, Speaker Cards, or Overlays.
3
Click Copy OBS Browser Source URL (default: http://localhost:8420/overlay.html).
4
In OBS Studio or vMix, add a Browser Source, paste the URL, and set width to 1920 and height to 1080.
5
In Stagelite, click TAKE ON AIR on any graphic. The overlay animates seamlessly over live video with crisp transparency.

Presenter Stage Display (Confidence Monitor)

Place a TV on the stage floor facing the speakers and worship band. The Stage Display shows:

  • Current Slide Lyrics: High-contrast, large text readable from 30 feet away.
  • Next Slide Preview: Shows the first line of the upcoming slide so the band knows what comes next.
  • Digital Clock & Countdown: Real-time wall clock and remaining sermon countdown.
  • Private Stage Messages: Operator notes (e.g. "Please wrap up in 2 minutes") that the audience cannot see.

Smartphone Remote Control

Control slides from anywhere in the auditorium using your iPhone, iPad, or Android phone:

  1. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi network as the presentation computer.
  2. In Stagelite, open Preferences (⌘,) to view the remote server IP and 4-digit security PIN.
  3. Open Safari or Chrome on your phone, visit the address, and enter the PIN to advance slides wirelessly.

Crash Recovery & Power Loss Safety

Instant Session Recovery
Stagelite saves every state change to SQLite immediately. If power is interrupted, relaunch Stagelite and click Resume Session on the amber banner. Your exact run-sheet, slide position, and screen windows are restored in under 2 seconds.

Stagelite Operator Manual Section 6: Master Keyboard Cheatsheet

6. Master Keyboard Cheatsheet

Print this sheet and tape it beside your presentation keyboard for instant reference during live events:

Shortcut Key Mode / Context What It Does
Space or → Live Presentation Advance to Next Slide in the active show
← Live Presentation Return to Previous Slide
B or F1 Live Presentation Toggle Blank Screen: Fades audience display to black immediately
Esc Live Presentation Clear Screen: Clears lyrics/scripture and returns to background
Enter Studio Mode TAKE: Sends staged preview slide live to the audience screen
⌘1 / Ctrl+1 Global Switch to Master Studio Mode (Primary control console)
⌘2 / Ctrl+2 Global Switch to Displays Mode (Hardware monitor verification)
⌘3 / Ctrl+3 Global Switch to Planning Mode (Service preparation)
⌘4 / Ctrl+4 Global Switch to WYSIWYG Slide Editor (Formatting & lyrics authoring)
⌘5 / Ctrl+5 Global Switch to Presenter View (Minimalist volunteer view)
⌘, / Ctrl+, Global Open Preferences & Settings
⌘↩ / Ctrl+Enter Slide Editor Publish Edits: Push editor changes live to audience display
⌘N / Ctrl+N Planning Create a new Program / Run-Sheet
⌘S / Ctrl+S Slide Editor Save changes to SQLite database
AV Desk Checklist Before Every Service:
  • [ ] Computer connected to external projector or video switcher via HDMI/DisplayPort.
  • [ ] Projector turned on and set to correct input.
  • [ ] Stagelite launched and Audience Window opened (● ON AIR badge active).
  • [ ] All service songs and scripture verified in the Service Schedule.
  • [ ] Test pattern (Identify) flashed to verify monitor alignment.
  • [ ] Panic hotkeys tested (B for Blank, Space for Next).
